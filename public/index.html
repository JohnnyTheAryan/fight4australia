What We Stand For

A grassroots movement of Australians who refuse to watch our nation, culture and future be sold off.

🛡️

National Identity

Restore pride in Australian heritage, history and culture. Our identity is not a museum piece — it is the foundation we pass to the next generation.

🛂

Sovereign Borders

End unsuitable mass immigration. Australia is our home, not an economic zone for international capital. Demand remigration and prioritise our people.

🗣️

Free Speech

Defend the right to speak freely. Reject the uniparty’s censorship laws that silence dissent and punish political expression.

⚔️

Against Woke Ideology

Stand firm against foreign agendas and cultural Marxism that seek to divide Australians and erase our foundations.

🤝

Community Power

Build real networks of Australians who organise, march, sticker, volunteer and refuse to be silenced. Grassroots strength over elite control.

🇦🇺

Australia First

Every policy, every decision, every fight must put the Australian people first. No more apologies for existing.
