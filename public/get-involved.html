Other Ways to Contribute

01

Create content

Videos, graphics, memes, write-ups. Share them through the movement’s channels. Original local content is especially valuable.

02

Local organising

Form or join a local team. Meet-ups, sticker runs, and building community offline are the foundation of long-term strength.

03

Amplify on social media

Follow the official accounts, share posts, and turn on notifications. Algorithm suppression is real — consistent sharing helps cut through it.

04

Stay informed

Keep up with the March for Australia channel and Fight for Australia socials so you know when the next actions are happening.
