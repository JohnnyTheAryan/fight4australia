Our Mission

Who we are and what we are fighting for

Fight for Australia is a grassroots nationalist movement of ordinary Australians who refuse to accept the managed decline of our country.

We are not a political party. We are not funded by foreign interests or corporate donors. We are volunteers, workers, parents and young people who still believe Australia is worth defending — and that the Australian people come first.

What We Believe

Australia is for the Australian people

Our country is not an economic zone or a hotel for the world. Mass immigration on the current scale is transforming Australia without the consent of the Australian people. We demand an end to unsuitable immigration and a policy of remigration that prioritises the interests of those already here.

Free speech is non-negotiable

Labor and the Liberals have passed wave after wave of laws restricting political expression. These laws have already been used against politicians, commentators, parties and news outlets. Australia is being pushed toward a UK-style system where ordinary people are arrested for social media posts. We reject this. Freedom of speech must be restored and protected.

Our identity is not up for negotiation

Australian heritage, history, culture and symbols are under constant attack from activists and institutions that treat our past as something to be erased. We reject the rewriting of our history and the guilt narrative that seeks to make Australians ashamed of their own country. Pride is not hate.

Community is power

Real change does not come from waiting for politicians. It comes from Australians organising, marching, stickering, creating content, and building networks that the establishment cannot ignore. We are building that network.

Our Demands

  1. End mass immigration — Return immigration to sustainable levels that serve the Australian people, not business lobbies.
  2. Remigration — Deport those who should not be here and prioritise Australians in housing, jobs and services.
  3. Repeal speech laws — Remove the legislation that has been used to silence political dissent and ban parties.
  4. Abolish the eSafety Commissioner — End government censorship of online discourse.
  5. Constitutional free speech — Hold a referendum to enshrine freedom of speech in the Australian Constitution.
  6. Australia First in all policy — Housing, infrastructure, culture and foreign policy must put Australians first.

How We Operate

We are a decentralised, volunteer-driven movement. Local organisers, stickering teams, content creators and march attendees are the backbone. We coordinate through social media and encrypted channels, and we work closely with the March for Australia campaign — the largest grassroots nationalist street actions in the country.

We do not do violence. We do not do foreign flags. We show up proudly, peacefully, and in numbers.
