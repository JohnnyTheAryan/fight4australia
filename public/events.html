NATIONWIDE

March for Australia

30 August 2026

Defend free speech. Reject uniparty censorship. Stand for the Australian people.

Labor and the Liberals are legislating away our right to disagree with them. Australia is on the path toward a UK-style police state where thousands are arrested for social media posts every year. The biggest threat to our freedom is no longer abroad — it is from our own government.

On 30 August we march in every capital city to demand the repeal of speech laws, the abolition of the eSafety Commissioner, and constitutional protection for free speech.

On the Day

  • • Bring the Australian flag. No foreign flags.
  • • Arrive early. Follow police instructions and stay with the crowd.
  • • Demonstrate peacefully and proudly.
  • • Bring family and friends. Numbers matter.
  • • Check local transport — road closures and delays are common on march days.
