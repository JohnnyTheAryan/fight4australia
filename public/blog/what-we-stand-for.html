Statement

What We Stand For

Fight for Australia is a grassroots nationalist movement. We are not a political party. We are volunteers who believe Australia is worth defending and that the Australian people come first.

Australian Identity

Our heritage, history, culture and symbols are not optional extras. They are the foundation we pass to the next generation. We reject the guilt narrative and the constant attempt to erase or rewrite who we are.

Sovereign Borders

Australia is our home, not an economic zone for international capital. Mass immigration on the current scale is transforming the country without consent. We demand sustainable levels that serve Australians, and a serious policy of remigration.

Free Speech

The right to speak, organise and disagree is being stripped away by the uniparty. We demand the repeal of speech laws, the abolition of the eSafety Commissioner, and constitutional protection for free speech.

Australia First

Housing, jobs, infrastructure, culture and foreign policy must put the Australian people first. No more apologies for existing.

Full details of our mission and demands are on the About page.
