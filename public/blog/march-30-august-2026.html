Nationwide March Confirmed — 30 August 2026

Fight for Australia and March for Australia confirm the next nationwide action will take place on Sunday 30 August 2026.

Australians will march in every capital city to defend free speech and reject the uniparty’s expanding censorship regime.

Labor and the Liberals have already used new laws against politicians, commentators, parties and news outlets. The path Australia is on leads toward a UK-style system where ordinary people are arrested for social media posts. We will not accept that future.

Locations

  • Melbourne — Flinders Street Station Steps, 12:00–14:00
  • Sydney — Belmore Park, Haymarket, 12:00–14:00
  • Brisbane — Roma Street Parklands, 12:00–14:00
  • Adelaide — Rundle Park, 12:00–14:00
  • Perth — Langley Park, 13:00–15:00
  • Canberra — Parliament House Lawns, 12:00–14:00

Full details are on the Events page. Register for updates so you don’t miss local information.

Bring the Australian flag. No foreign flags. Come peaceful, proud, and in numbers.
